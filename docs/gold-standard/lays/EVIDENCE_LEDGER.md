# Lay's Gold Standard — Internal Evidence Ledger

INTERNAL. Not published. Classifies the important claims/metrics behind the Lay's
Gold Standard graph so the public Drafts stay honest and scope-safe, and so the
future Case Study Agent has a benchmark to reproduce.

**Classification:** `FACT` (directly evidenced) · `SYNTHESIS` (combined from
multiple sources) · `INFERENCE` (reasoned, not directly stated) · `MISSING`
(claimed but unverifiable here) · `CONFLICT` (sources disagree).

**Metric scope tags:** Geek-managed activation · Whole campaign · PR/media ·
Brand collaboration · Point-in-time · Final result · Estimated · Tracked ·
Retrospective.

**Confidentiality:** Private first-party sources (2020 PepsiCo email, Drive
briefs/reports/sheets) may VALIDATE a public-safe fact without being exposed. No
email contents, commercials, rate cards, personal contacts or negotiations are
reproduced anywhere public.

## Metric-integrity rules applied
- Geek-managed activation, whole-campaign, PR/media, brand-partner and follower-base
  figures are **never merged**.
- Estimated / perceived creator media value is **never** labelled audited ROI.
- On conflict (Smile, MyLaysRelationchip) figures are **recorded, not averaged**;
  the public Draft shows the defensible public wider-campaign figure and/or the
  Geek-scope figure with an explicit label.
- Unverifiable metrics are **withheld** from public Drafts and marked MISSING here.
